Float Prompter

Your script in a floating window while you film in another app.

Preview

Tap Float it, then swipe to TikTok or your camera.

How to test it
  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Float it. The small window pops out.
  3. Swipe home and open TikTok, Instagram, or Camera. The window should stay on top and keep scrolling.
  4. If the text freezes once you leave Safari, come back and tap Make it a video. It records the scroll first, then floats a real video, which iPhone never pauses.
  5. Drag the window near the camera lens so your eyes look natural.
